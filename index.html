Features

Everything you need for daily Islamic practice, learning, and community engagement — in one lightweight app.

📅

Islamic Calendar

Auto-updating Islamic (Hijri) calendar with accurate dates. Requires an internet connection to keep dates synchronized and current.

📖

Quran Integration

Access the Holy Quran directly within the app with online features for reading and exploration in a clean, accessible format.

🤲

Duas & Aamal

A growing collection of daily supplications (duas) and practices (aamal) for various occasions, presented in Gujarati text.

🎵

Kalams & Poetry

Browse a curated collection of Islamic kalams (poetic verses) celebrating faith, devotion, and spirituality.

🎤

Noha & Majalis

Access texts and content related to Nohas and Majalis for community gatherings and religious commemorations.

⚡

Lightweight & Fast

Optimized for quick installs and smooth performance. Regular bug fixes and updates keep the experience reliable.

Daily Use Companion

ILM is designed for everyday use — from checking the Islamic date each morning, to reading daily duas, exploring the Quran, or finding the right kalam for an occasion. It's your pocket companion for Islamic content in Gujarati.
